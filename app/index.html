    Roadmap to the platinum

    One playthrough is enough, and there is no difficulty setting and no mission replay — free roam carries on after the story, and the full 100% checklist opens once the credits have rolled. Published guides put the whole run at about 200 hours. Work the stages in order; the checklist above tracks every trophy in them.
